The SVG is sized in millimetres, so it opens at the right physical size in Illustrator, Inkscape or Affinity.

Where the data comes from. Positions are the Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 9,096 stars down to about magnitude 7 — everything a naked eye can see and a good deal more. Coordinates are J2000 and are precessed to your chosen date before plotting.

What is and isn't modelled. Trails are true small circles on the celestial sphere, projected stereographically, so each one is an exact circular arc rather than a sampled polyline. Atmospheric refraction, light pollution and proper motion are ignored; none of them is visible at print scale.

Before printing. Convert caption text to outlines in your layout tool, since the SVG references fonts by name rather than embedding them. Add bleed if your printer asks for it.
