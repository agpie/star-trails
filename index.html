Print check

    Where the data comes from. Star positions are the Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 9,096 stars down to about magnitude 7. Constellation figures come from johanley/constellation-lines, released under CC0, so they carry no attribution or licence obligation. J2000 coordinates are precessed to your chosen date before plotting.

    What is and isn't modelled. Trails are true small circles on the celestial sphere, projected stereographically, so each is an exact circular arc rather than a sampled polyline. The Milky Way is a schematic glow along the galactic plane, faded near the horizon the way atmospheric extinction fades it in life. Refraction, light pollution and proper motion are ignored.

    Fonts. The PDF carries subset copies of Newsreader and Karla embedded inside it, so captions print exactly as they appear here and no printer can substitute anything. Both faces are Open Font License, which permits embedding in documents; the licences travel inside the font data. The SVG and the PNG carry the same subset fonts inline, so all three exports set type identically.
